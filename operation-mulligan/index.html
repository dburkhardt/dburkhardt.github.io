01 / DANIEL · ADÁN · SAM · ROBERT · PAULTAMPA, FLORIDAWINTER 2027

The mission briefing.

Read. Pack. Show up.
01 / What

A “golf” trip.

Four days of friends, sunshine, drinks, and wildly optimistic attempts at golf. Talent is neither expected nor particularly welcome.

02 / Where

Tampa, Florida.

Warm-weather intentions. Cold drinks. Somewhere a long way from our normal responsibilities. House and courses to be decided.

03 / When

Winter 2027.

Four days. Dates to be locked in together. For now, consider this your official heads-up.

The only ground rule

Zero golf ability required.

We're not here
to get good at golf.

We're here for the hang. Warm weather, alcohol, drugs, long dinners, and laughing at each other's swings. Whatever your version of a good time looks like, nobody has to keep up. The scorecard is optional. The company is the whole point.

A very loose game plan.

Tentative. Obviously.
01

Arrive. Exhale.

Get to Tampa. Settle in. First drinks, a good dinner, and deeply unearned confidence.

02

Meet the fairway.

A little golf. A lot of mulligans. Poolside recovery from our athletic achievements.

03

Run it back.

More golf if we feel like it. More hanging out regardless. One properly good night.

04

Debrief. Disappear.

Slow breakfast. Compare sunburns. Head home with stories and no measurable improvement.

Mission status: yours to accept

Clear four days.
Bring zero expectations.

You in? Tell the group chat.
